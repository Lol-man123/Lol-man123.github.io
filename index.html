AutoUploader

AutoUploader is a personal publishing tool. It edits short videos and posts them to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts on a schedule, then reads those accounts' video statistics to decide what to make next.

It is used only by its owner, for the owner's own accounts. It does not offer sign-ups or serve other users.

AutoUploader uses YouTube API Services. Use of it is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
