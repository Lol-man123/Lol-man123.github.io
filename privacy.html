Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

AutoUploader is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own social media accounts. This policy explains what it accesses and how that data is handled.

What it accesses

  • YouTube (through YouTube API Services): uploading videos and thumbnails, reading basic channel information, and reading video statistics and YouTube Analytics for the owner's channels.
  • Instagram: publishing Reels and reading basic profile information and post insights for the owner's accounts.
  • TikTok: uploading and publishing videos and reading the owner's video list and statistics.

How the data is used and stored

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are used solely to publish the owner's videos and to measure how those videos perform. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Video files are kept briefly in private cloud storage while Instagram downloads them, then deleted.

Google and YouTube

AutoUploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page.

Deleting data

Deleting the AutoUploader folder from the owner's computer removes all stored tokens and statistics. Revoking access in each platform's settings stops all further access.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email shown on the app's sign-in screen.
